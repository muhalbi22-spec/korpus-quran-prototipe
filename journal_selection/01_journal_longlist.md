# 01 · Journal longlist

Prepared: 28 October 2026. Manuscript: "Between Form and Faith: Equivalence and the Semantic Narrowing of Taqwā, Raḥmat, and Kāfir in the Quranic Arabic Corpus" (desk-declined by *New Voices in Translation Studies*, 11 October 2026, with an invitation to resubmit after six months).

## How the longlist was built

1. **Manuscript first.** I read the submitted article (5,990 words including references, below the 6,000–8,000 range the editor asked for) and the full Master's thesis (535 tokens across five terms). I then re-checked the article's data against the Quranic Arabic Corpus. The findings are in `00_manuscript_and_data_audit.md`.
2. **Disciplinary positioning.** The article is best positioned as **descriptive Translation Studies**: corpus-informed analysis of a documentary (interlinear) translation layer, with sacred-text and religious-translation relevance. Corpus linguistics and semantics journals were screened but rank lower, because the study does not develop corpus methodology or semantic theory as such (see `04_top_three_strategy.md`).
3. **Search sources.** Official journal sites where reachable (jostrans.org, newvoices.arts.chula.ac.th, journals.uco.es, erudit.org, aljamiah.or.id), the DOAJ API (fee fields), and web search for pages this environment could not open. tandfonline.com, benjamins.com / jbe-platform.com, euppublishing.com, akjournals.com and scimagojr.com all returned HTTP 403 Cloudflare bot challenges, so for those journals I could only see search-engine excerpts of the official pages plus third-party aggregators. Evidence levels are defined in `02_fee_and_quartile_verification.md`.

## What the screening showed

- **Q1 depends on the category.** Several translation journals are Q1 only in *Literature and Literary Theory* and Q2 in *Linguistics and Language*. Hikma (official journal page) and New Voices (aggregator) both follow this pattern for 2025. The ranking tables say which category each quartile refers to.
- **The free route is mostly the subscription route.** The large translation journals (Taylor & Francis, Benjamins, Akadémiai, EUP) are hybrid. Publishing without open access carries no fee, and OA is optional and paid: Benjamins lists EUR 1,800 excl. tax. The diamond options with no fee at all are Hikma, JoSTrans, New Voices and Al-Jāmiʿah.
