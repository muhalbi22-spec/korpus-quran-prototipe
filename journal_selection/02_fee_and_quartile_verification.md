# 02 · Fee and quartile verification

All checks: **28 October 2026**. Quartiles depend on the subject category and the year. The latest SJR release is 2025 (published mid-2026).

## Evidence levels

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **V1** | Official page or official registry (journal site, DOAJ API) opened and read directly in this session |
| **V2** | Official page seen only as a search-engine excerpt. Its URL is official, but I could not open it (publisher returned HTTP 403 / Cloudflare challenge) |
| **V3** | Third-party aggregator (ResearchJournalRank, SciJournal, Resurchify, Researcher.Life, Journal Metrics) or a university library record. Indicative only |
| **—** | Not found |

SCImago (scimagojr.com) and Scopus Sources could not be opened, so **no SJR quartile here is V1 from SCImago itself**. The one V1 quartile is the journal's own statement of its SJR 2025 ranking (Hikma). A 5-minute manual check is in `06_sources_and_verification_dates.md`.

## Fee terms used

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Free submission | No fee to submit. Says nothing about publication. |
| No mandatory APC (subscription route) | Hybrid journal. Publishing behind the paywall costs nothing; OA is optional and paid. |
| Diamond OA | Open access with no author fees at any stage. |
| Optional gold OA | Paid OA in a hybrid journal. |
| Conditional waiver | Fee reduced or removed by institutional deal, Research4Life status, etc. |

## Update after the expert review (28 Oct 2026)

**SJR vs CiteScore.** "Scopus Q1" can mean two different things:
- the **SJR quartile**, computed by SCImago from Scopus data;
- Scopus's own **CiteScore quartile/percentile**.

Most institutions mean SJR. For Perspectives, the publisher page shows a Q1 **CiteScore** (V2), which is the most direct evidence of "Scopus Q1" for that title. Record both measures in the manual check.
