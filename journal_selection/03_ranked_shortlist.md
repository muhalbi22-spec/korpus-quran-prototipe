**What "Scopus Q1" means here.** Two quartile measures exist.
- **SJR quartile.** SCImago computes it from Scopus data, and it is what most institutions mean by "Q1 SJR".
- **CiteScore quartile.** This is Scopus's own percentile measure, shown on Scopus source pages and on many publisher pages.

The two can differ. Record **both** when you check manually (see `06`).
