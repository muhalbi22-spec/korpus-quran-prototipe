# 04 · Top-three strategy (updated after expert review)

Updated 28 October 2026. These are recommendations, not facts. Nothing here predicts or promises acceptance.

## Positioning

Position the article primarily as **Translation Studies**: descriptive, corpus-informed study of sacred-text translation. The object of study is a translation layer, the QAC interlinear gloss, compared with five sentence-level translations. Linguistics and semantics (Baker, Izutsu) are the analytical tools, not the topic.

This is also why the article fits Hikma. Hikma's own "Focus and Scope" says that since 2017 the journal has been limited **exclusively to Translation and Interpreting**, including contrastive studies and humanistic, legal, scientific and other specialised fields. The "Literature & Literary Theory" label on its Q1 is SCImago's classification of the journal, not a restriction on article topics.

## Recommended top three

### 1. First choice: *Hikma: Revista de Traducción*

**Why first:**
- Q1 and no fees are both verified at V1. Its own page states SJR 2025 Q1 (Literature & Literary Theory), and DOAJ records no APC and no other charges.
- The scope is translation studies only.
- English is accepted.
- The manuscript is already built in Hikma's English template: title in English and Spanish, abstracts of 271 (EN) and 292 (ES) words, 5 keywords, APA 7, tables captioned below, an Ethics Statement, and 8,304 words in total (limit 7,000–9,000 including references).

**Window:** **15 January – 15 February 2027.** The next window is 15 September – 15 October 2027.

**Before submitting:** see `05_first_choice_revision_checklist.md`. In particular:
- rewrite the text in your own words and complete the AI statement truthfully;
- prepare the Word file listing three possible reviewers;
- register your ORCID;
- fill in the ethics section ("No aplica");
- upload the **anonymised** version.

### 2. Second choice: *Cadernos de Tradução* (UFSC)

**Why:**
- Same verified profile as Hikma: SJR 2025 Q1 in Literature & Literary Theory per its 28 April 2026 announcement, and no fees of any kind (V1).
- Accepts English.
- Up to 10,000 words excluding references.
- APA 7.

**What must change:**
- Move the text into the Cadernos template (Gill Sans MT; abstract 150–300 words; 3–5 keywords separated by semicolons).
- Check whether the next issue is open or thematic before submitting.

### 3. Third choice: *Perspectives* or *The Translator* (Taylor & Francis), only after the gate check

**Why:**
- Strongest Linguistics & Language readership.
- Perspectives shows a Q1 CiteScore and SJR 2025 = 0.935 on its publisher page (V2).
- The Translator states "no APC" if you do not choose open access (V2).

**Gate check:** both need a manual SCImago/CiteScore check and a reading of the author instructions, including the AI policy and the confirmation of no APC.

**What must change:** reformat to Taylor & Francis style, and remove the Spanish abstract.

### Backup: invited resubmission to *New Voices in Translation Studies*

**Why it is a strong backup:**

**Constraints:**
